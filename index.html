Food Bridge

The app creates a seamless bridge between food providers (event caterers, restaurants) and food distributors (NGOs, volunteers) to ensure surplus food reaches the needy instead of being wasted.

Self Project 1 Month UI/UX · Mobile App
Problem statement

Food goes to waste while people go hungry.

A significant amount of food from weddings and large events goes to waste, while many people struggle with hunger. There is a need for a streamlined, reliable system that connects event caterers with NGOs to efficiently donate and distribute surplus food to those in need.
30% Food wastage in weddings

Food wastage is a significant issue at weddings, when food served goes uneaten, often due to over-preparation, buffet culture, social pressure, fear of scarcity, and lack of planning.

27.3 Global Hunger Index score

India ranks 105th out of 127 countries with sufficient data to calculate GHI scores, with a score of 27.3, indicating a "serious" level of hunger.

224M Undernourished people in India

According to the United Nations Food and Agriculture Organization, there were approximately 224 million undernourished people living throughout India as of 2021.

Solution

AnnSetu app: real-time food donation requests, smart matching algorithm, volunteer network, food safety compliance, recognition & incentives.

User persona

Two sides of the bridge.

RM

Ravi Mehta, 43 y.o

Owner of Mehta Caterer · Food donor
Goals
  • Reduce food waste while ensuring sufficient supply.
  • Maintain consistent taste & hygiene standards.
Pain points
  • Less shelf-life food has to be consumed in 2–3 days.
  • No consistent place for donating the food.
  • Proof of distribution amongst the needy, if food is donated.
AS

Anita Sharma, 35 y.o

Volunteer at an NGO · Food distributor
Goals
  • Find consistent food donors to serve more needy people.
  • Ensure timely pickup & delivery to prevent food spoilage.
Pain points
  • Food quality and hygiene.
  • No easy way to find food donations.
  • Struggles with transportation.
  • Uncertainty about the food quantity.
Features & specifications

What the app does.

01

User Registration

  • Role-based sign-up (Caterer/Restaurant, NGO, Volunteer)
  • Business verification for food providers
  • NGO verification to ensure legitimacy
  • Profile management (edit info, donation history, ratings)
02

Food Donation & Requests

  • Food providers can list available food with details:
    • Type of food, quantity
    • Expiry time, storage requirements
  • NGOs can browse & request donations
  • AI-powered matching: auto-connects NGOs with nearby food providers
  • Scheduled & instant donations based on need
03

Food Safety & Quality Control

  • Automated food safety checklist before listing
  • NGO approval system: accept/reject food based on quality
  • Food condition ratings & feedback for transparency
04

Location & Delivery

  • Volunteer matching system: assign nearest available volunteer
  • Real-time tracking: NGOs & donors track pickup & delivery
  • Live notifications & alerts for all users
  • Delivery & pick-up mode
05

AI-Powered Smart Insights

  • Predictive analytics: suggest donation frequency based on past behaviour
06

Recognition

  • Badges & rewards for top donors & volunteers
User flow & wireframe

Two kinds of users, one simple flow.

Food donors and food distributors share one journey: onboarding → login or sign up (personal details, organisation verification) → schedule or request a food donation → match meals → track the meal → upload proof → feedback & rating.
